How to choose a better background subtraction result
=================================================

Prefer the result that satisfies all of these:
1. Background median/mean/p95 are low and background_black_pct is high.
2. CNR_mean is higher than before.
3. Foreground_p95 retention is not too low; as a rough guide, avoid <0.35 unless you intentionally want a very sparse signal.
4. Sharpness retention is not too low; low sharpness means structures may be smoothed/erased.
5. The removed-signal image should look like smooth background, not like the organoid/ring.
6. Avoid outputs flagged as grey-baseline/artifact or background-not-clean.

Summary flags:
- before: OK / plausible
- 1: OK / plausible
- 0.75: OK / plausible

Key metric table is in summary_comparison.csv.
